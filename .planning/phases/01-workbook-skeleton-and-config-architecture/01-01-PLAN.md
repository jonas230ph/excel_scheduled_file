<threat_model>
## Trust Boundaries

- Local workbook generation script writes to the project workspace only.
- Excel formulas and named ranges are local workbook content, not executable macros.
- Phase 1 must not introduce VBA, Office Scripts, network calls, credential storage, or external integrations.

## STRIDE Register

| Threat ID | Category | Component | Disposition | Mitigation Plan |
|-----------|----------|-----------|-------------|-----------------|
| T1 | Tampering | `Config_Settings` activity rules | Mitigated | Keep activity rules visible and table-driven; protect formulas later while leaving config cells editable. |
| T2 | Information Disclosure | Workbook contents | Accepted | Phase 1 uses sample non-sensitive data only; no PII import or external sharing is introduced. |
| T3 | Denial of Service | Workbook recalculation | Mitigated | Generate selected-day scoped structure; do not create full weekly wall-chart formulas in Phase 1. |
| T4 | Elevation of Privilege | Macros/scripts inside workbook | Mitigated | Do not add VBA or macro-enabled `.xlsm`; generate plain `.xlsx`. |
</threat_model>
